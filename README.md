# Nexus Guilds

**Found a town. Raise the guilds. Build it your way.**

Nexus Guilds is a modern colony mod for NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.1. Recruit colonists, raise guild halls, and grow your settlement from a camp into a city. It's built to run light, even in big modpacks, and to work with the machines and storage from the mods you already use.

> **Status:** in planning. Development comes after [Khaos Nexus](https://github.com/Khaos-Krew/Khaos-Nexus-Mod). It will be released on CurseForge. Ports to newer versions come once 1.21.1 is stable.

---

## Features (planned)

### Built to run light
- Colonists run full AI only when players are nearby. Colonies with no player around keep producing, calculated in efficient batches instead of being simulated tick by tick.
- Servers can cap colony size in the config.

### Building your town
- Builders construct core buildings from schematics.
- Everything else is up to you: build rooms however you like and mark them for use.

### Guild halls
- Each job has its own guild hall, like the Builders' Guild, the Miners' Guild and the Machinists' Guild.
- Upgrade a hall to unlock that job's recipes, tools and perks, and to raise your colonists' skill caps. There's no single research tree.
- Guilds post **contracts** (deliveries, defense and more) that pay Renown and guild reputation.

### Renown
- Your colony grows by earning **Renown**, which raises the colonist cap and unlocks buildings.
- You earn Renown from trade, finished buildings, and defeating bosses near your town.

### Colonists
- First-release jobs are Builder, Farmer, Miner, Lumberjack, Courier, Warehouse keeper, Guard and Machinist, with more coming later.
- Colonists level up by working, up to the cap set by their guild hall.
- Their needs are kept light: food, housing, and a happiness score that affects how fast they work.
- Choose your colony's look in game, from player-style skins to fantasy races like humans, elves and dwarves, and override the look of individual colonists.

### Colony tablet
- Manage your whole colony remotely from a handheld tablet.

### Play together
- Each player gets one colony, and teammates (FTB Teams) can build it up together.

## Compatibility (planned)
Nexus Guilds never needs any other mod. Each one below just adds more when it's installed.
- **Machines:** colonists can run other mods' machines through standard item, fluid and energy hooks.
- **Storage networks:** AE2, Refined Storage, Create, Mekanism and more.
- **FTB Teams and FTB Quests**
- **[Khaos Nexus](https://github.com/Khaos-Krew/Khaos-Nexus-Mod):** gate breaks threaten your town, and Guards defend it. Clearing gates near your city earns Renown, resources and recruits, and the colony can trade through the Gate Board.

Want another mod supported? [Request mod support](https://github.com/Khaos-Krew/Nexus-Guilds/issues/new?template=mod_support_request.yml), and give a 👍 to requests you'd like to see.

## Reporting issues
Found a bug, a crash, or a mod conflict? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Khaos-Krew/Nexus-Guilds/issues/new/choose) and pick the matching form. Please include your mod version, modpack, and an [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs) log link.
